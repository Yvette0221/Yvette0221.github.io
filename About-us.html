About Yvia

Yvia is your one-step online matketplace for brand-new goods and second-hand items. You can brownse our new products, or find affordable used treasures from other users.

Our store name Yvia comes from combining Yvette and Lucia, the two co-creators of this platform. We designed our original brand logo, and chose teal as our theme color to represent freshness and creativity.

This website is one of the most popular shopping project in ICS3U. Yvia is more than just an class idea--it is our self-built digital marketplace where new items and second-hand finds coexist.

We believe good items can keep being loved. Whether you want to buy brand-new products, or hunt for unique second-hand bargains, Yvia is the place for you

About Our Logo

Our Yvia logo combines the letters Y, V, I and A into one unified graphic. The left cyan-white graphic is Y, made up of V on top and I below. The right-side cyan, white and light blue shapes form A. The overlapping letters represent the partnership of co-founders Yvette and Lucia.
